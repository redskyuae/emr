# Asset Location and Custodian Relations Are Free-Text For Now

An Asset is conceptually located at a Facility, serves a Department, and is overseen by a Custodian (a Staff member), as recorded in `CONTEXT.md`. However, the system does not yet model Facility or Department as tables, and binding Custodian to Staff would couple the Asset module to Staff provisioning before it is needed. We are therefore storing `facility`, `department`, `location`, and `custodian` as plain free-text columns on the Asset rather than foreign keys. This unblocks Asset inventory CRUD immediately without waiting on the Facility, Department, and Staff-linking work, and it matches the shape of the existing hard-coded asset mock data.

The trade-off is that these fields carry no referential integrity: typos and inconsistent names are possible, and cross-entity queries (e.g. "all Assets in the Cardiology Department") are string-matched rather than joined. By contrast, Asset Category, Asset Status, and Asset Condition are real Tenant-scoped Master tables and ARE referenced from the Asset by integer foreign keys, because those Masters are built in the same batch.

This is a deliberately reversible decision. When the Facility and Department Masters and a Custodian→Staff link are introduced, a dedicated migration task should add the foreign-key columns, backfill them from the existing free-text values, and then drop or deprecate the free-text columns. Until that migration runs, `facility`, `department`, `location`, and `custodian` remain free text, and `custodian` may be null to represent an unassigned Asset.
