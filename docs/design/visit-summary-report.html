Visit Summary Report

A printable summary of one Visit: who the Patient is, who saw them and when, what was found, what was diagnosed and planned, what treatment was given, and what was billed. It is produced from a single Visit and can be handed to the Patient or kept on file.

2 · Plan

Summary plan

One summary, two copies. The Patient copy is what the Patient takes home. The Clinical copy adds history, audit detail and documents for the file, a referral, or another Doctor.

Never printed

  • Emirates ID, passport and other identifiers. MRN and name are enough to match the record.
  • VIP and Medical Tourist flags. Internal handling flags, not part of the clinical record.
  • Draft notes. Only signed notes describe what the Doctor stands behind.
  • File URLs. Blob links would hand out documents to anyone holding the paper.

Build outline

  • GET /api/v1/visits/[id]/summary returns the whole summary in one response, with a types.ts and Swagger entry. Billing is dropped server-side without permission.
  • One query, get-visit-summary-query.ts, in the visit module, reading through existing repositories; every read filters by tenantId.
  • Screen at app/(protected)/visits/[id]/summary/ with a page-shaped loader.tsx; copy type and billing toggle live in the URL via nuqs.
  • The PDF is rendered from the same response, so the screen and the file never disagree.

4 · Before building

Open decisions

Gaps found in the schema that change what the summary can say.

Medicines prescribed in the Visit are not recorded

Patient medications are a Patient-level list with no link to a Visit, so the summary can only print current medications, not "prescribed today". If the clinic hands Patients a prescription with this summary, a Visit-linked prescription is a separate feature.

Allergies and medications are printed as of today

They are not snapshotted per Visit, so reprinting an old Visit shows today's lists. The sheet labels them "As on <print date>". Confirm that is acceptable for a historical record.

No follow-up instruction field

The Plan holds only Treatment precautions. The summary shows the next booked Appointment instead. Decide whether the consultation form should gain a "Review after" field.

Letterhead has no Facility address

Visits do not record a Facility, and the Tenant holds only a name and logo. Until a Facility is on the Visit, the letterhead prints the Tenant name and logo only.

Who can print which copy

Proposed: Visit read permission prints the Patient copy; Clinical copy needs the clinical read permission used by the Visit workspace; billing needs Billing permission. Confirm the permission names.
